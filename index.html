BEHIND THE BLOOM

Simple to use. Real AI inside.

No terminal or programming required. The browser does the image comparison for you.

01 — CAPTURE

Choose a flower photo

Pick a clear image from your phone or computer, preferably with one flower in focus.

02 — COMPARE

A visual model checks it

A pre-trained CLIP model compares your photo with descriptions of the selected flower types.

03 — DISCOVER

See the likely result

Review the top match and alternative matches. If the flower is outside the list, add a candidate name.

An educational photo classifier powered by a pre-trained CLIP model. This is a different, image-based approach from the three-species Iris measurement project. Results are indicative, not botanical verification.
